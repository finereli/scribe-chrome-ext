Scribe

Transcribes your browser calls, Meet and WhatsApp included, with your own OpenAI key. No bot joins.

1

Add your OpenAI key

Your microphone is "Me" in the transcript and the call tab is "Them". Each turn goes to OpenAI on your own key, about 30 cents per call-hour. Make a key

2

Let Scribe hear your microphone

Scribe records your side of the call from the microphone. The browser asks once, here, because the recorder runs in the background where it can't ask.

3

Pin Scribe and take a call

Pin Scribe from the puzzle-piece menu. When a Meet or WhatsApp call starts, Scribe offers to record it. On any other site, click the Scribe icon and press the red button. Alt+Shift+S opens it from the keyboard.
